# KnownText

**A more organized SMS/MMS app for Android.**

KnownText helps you focus on messages from people you know while keeping the rest of your inbox easy to review when you are ready. It is designed to be simple, private, and free of ads.

## What KnownText does

- Separates conversations into **Contacts** and **Other** inboxes.
- Lets you filter each inbox by **All**, **Unread**, or **Favorites**.
- Sends normal notifications for contacts while allowing other messages to remain quiet.
- Supports SMS and MMS, including group messages and media attachments.
- Provides search, drafts, message forwarding, emoji reactions, and quick copy actions for verification codes, phone numbers, and addresses.
- Lets you pin, mute, archive, block, or move conversations to Trash. Trashed conversations can be restored for up to 30 days.
- Includes dark mode, adjustable font size, and accessibility-minded controls.

## Important: default SMS app

KnownText needs to be selected as your Android device's **default SMS app** to send and receive messages and apply its notification filtering. Android will request this during setup. You can switch back to another SMS app at any time in Android Settings.

## Privacy

KnownText does not run a developer server and does not use ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Messages, contacts, and settings are processed on your device. Optional developer tips are handled by Google Play.

For the complete details, see the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

## Limitations

- **RCS is not supported.** Android does not provide third-party messaging apps with a public RCS API.
- MMS and group-message delivery depend on your carrier and typically require mobile data.
