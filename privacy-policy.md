# KnownText Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 20, 2026

KnownText is a default SMS/MMS application for Android. This policy explains how
the app handles information when you use it.

## Summary

KnownText does not operate a developer server, does not sell personal data, and
does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Your messages, contacts,
and filtering choices are processed and stored on your Android device.

## Information the app accesses

When you make KnownText the device's default SMS app and grant the relevant
Android permissions, it accesses the following information only to provide its
core messaging features:

- **SMS and MMS messages**, including their content, sender/recipient phone
  numbers, dates, delivery state, and attachments, to display, send, receive,
  search, filter, block, and manage conversations.
- **Contacts**, including contact names and profile images, to identify contacts
  in conversation lists and provide recipient suggestions.
- **Phone and subscription information**, such as the device's own phone numbers
  when available, to distinguish messages you send to yourself and select a
  suitable subscription for sending.
- **Notifications**, when you grant notification permission, to notify you about
  incoming messages according to your filtering settings.

KnownText accesses SMS and MMS permissions only while it is the device's active
default SMS application. You can revoke the default-SMS role or any permission
at any time in Android Settings; doing so will limit or stop the corresponding
features.

## How information is used and stored

Message data is read from and written to Android's system telephony provider so
that KnownText can function as your messaging app. Contact information is read
from Android's contacts provider. The app keeps its own small on-device settings,
such as sender state, group names, and Trash metadata, in the app's local storage.

The app does not transmit message content, phone numbers, contacts, attachments,
or filtering settings to the developer or to any third-party server. It does not
use this information to create advertising profiles or for cross-app tracking.

Conversations moved to Trash remain recoverable in the app for up to 30 days.
After that period, the app attempts to permanently remove the associated message
records from the device. You can restore or permanently delete a trashed
conversation earlier.

## Optional developer tips

KnownText offers optional one-time developer tips through Google Play Billing.
If you choose to make a tip, Google processes the payment under its own privacy
policy. KnownText receives only the purchase information needed to confirm and
consume that tip; it does not receive or store your payment-card or bank details.

## Sharing

KnownText does not share the information described above with the developer or
with third parties. The following platform services may process data as part of
the services you choose to use:

- Your mobile carrier processes SMS/MMS messages to deliver them.
- Google Play processes optional in-app purchases.
- Android and your device manufacturer may process data according to their own
  system services and settings.

## Security

The app relies on Android's app sandbox and system providers to protect locally
stored data. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely
secure; protect your device with a screen lock and keep Android up to date.

## Children

KnownText is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal
information from children through a developer-operated service.
